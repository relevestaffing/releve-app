## The three questions

### 1. Where Relève is genuinely ahead — and the differentiator claim tested

The Book's central claim is that **no agency in this category shows the client the working**. I tested it, and it holds:

- **Athena** matches algorithmically and shows the client nothing of the reasoning.
- **Persona** markets "personalities and work styles matched" but that is a backend claim, not a client-facing artifact — the buyer never sees it.
- **Somewhere** is recruiting-forward: a shortlist with résumés, no assessment shown.
- The category as a whole treats assessment as an *internal vetting tool*, never a deliverable.

So three of Relève's four differentiators are real and defensible, and I could find no competitor doing them:

1. **The Signature is taken by both sides**, and matches on how two specific people will work together, not on a CV. Nobody else assesses the client at all.
2. **The Signature Match is shown to the client** — the reasoning, the fit, the growth edge, as a named artifact. This is the single most distinctive thing Relève does, and it is exactly the thing a $3,500 buyer cannot get anywhere else.
3. **A real two-sided product.** Everyone else sells a service with, at most, a thin portal. Relève has an actual talent-facing product (assessments, Taking The Watch, payout, check-ins) and an actual client product (pulse, tasks, billing, self-serve notice). This is a genuine moat — and also where the launch risk lives, because it only counts if it works, which is what Parts C–F are for.

The fourth, **one candidate at a time**, is distinctive in combination (with reasons captured on both approve and decline) but weaker alone: Athena effectively hands you one match too. Relève's version is better because of #2, not on its own.

### 2. Where Relève is at parity, and it does not matter

The offshore cost advantage ("30% of the cost") is real but is table stakes — Somewhere says 77%, everyone offshore says 70%+. Do not lead with price against this field; lead with the Signature. Replacement guarantees and "fully managed" are parity too. None of these win or lose a deal.

### 3. Where Relève is BEHIND in a way a $3,500 buyer notices on a demo — ranked by deal risk

1. **No proof.** Every competitor shows named cases, numbers, testimonials, logos. Relève shows none. On a demo the buyer asks "who else uses you." This is the biggest deal-loser — but it is a cold-start problem, not a product gap, and the honest fix is founder story + the Signature Match as proof-of-rigor, never fabricated testimonials.
2. **No executive onboarding / delegation training.** This is *Athena's single biggest lever* (confirmed: a Delegation Training Library plus a structured intake and a 60-minute call). Relève has a 90-day plan for the talent and nothing structured for the executive. A buyer comparing directly will feel the gap. Buildable as curriculum, not headcount — the Book already flags it as an opportunity.
3. **No ROI / cost-comparison the buyer can see.** Somewhere and Athena both show "you save $58k/yr." Relève asserts "30% of the cost" without ever showing the buyer their own number. Cheap to build, closes the value gap on a call.
4. **Payments and signing are built but switched off.** Deposits are collected by hand until Stripe is live; agreements wait on DocuSign. On a demo this reads as unfinished. Operational, not product — needs credentials, not code.
5. **The marketing "how it works" is less legible than the category's.** Competitors lead with a dead-simple numbered path and a clear price. Relève's 14-day guarantee is stronger than anything they offer but is not framed as sharply.
